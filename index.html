Assumptions — fuel GCV uses standard operating-baseline (rule-of-thumb) as-fired values per fuel type: mesocarp fibre 10,000–11,500 kJ/kg at 35–40% moisture, palm kernel shell 14,500–16,500 kJ/kg at 15–20% moisture, shredded EFB 7,500–8,500 kJ/kg at 45–50% moisture — midpoints pre-filled, editable if you have a lab result. Combined fuel GCV is the mass-weighted blend of whichever streams are in use. Steam assumed saturated at the stated pressure (interpolated from steam tables); feedwater enthalpy from Cp = 4.186 kJ/kg·K. Fibre/shell/EFB yield ratios vary with fruit variety, press and depericarper efficiency — treat fuel-mass estimates as indicative, not metered.

Turbine & cogeneration — exhaust (process) steam is also assumed saturated at the stated back-pressure, so turbine + generator conversion is read off actual meter output against the enthalpy drop rather than a full isentropic/entropy calculation — adequate for a rule-of-thumb estimate, not a substitute for a proper turbine heat balance. Assumes all boiler steam passes through the turbine before reaching process (no direct let-down/bypass line). Useful process heat values the exhaust steam at its own enthalpy against feedwater, same basis as the boiler steam duty — it does not net off any condensate return, since palm oil mill sterilizers typically consume steam directly rather than returning condensate.
